About the studio

Razan Sheikh started painting on the walls of her own kitchen.

What began as an unpaid mural for a friend's café turned into Razan Arts — now a small studio taking on commissioned paintings, portraits, and murals for homes and businesses that want something made specifically for their space.

Every project starts the same way: a conversation, a sketch, and a palette chosen for the room it's going into — not a catalogue. The studio works in oil, acrylic, and large-scale wall paint, and takes on a limited number of commissions each season to keep the attention each one deserves.

Five ways to work with the studio

Custom Portraits

Hand-painted portraits in oil or acrylic — people, pets, and family pieces made from photos or a live sitting.

Wall Murals
